## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my Data Analyst Internship at **SWYNEX Technologies**.

The project combines three internship tasks into one end-to-end analytics case study:

**Data Cleaning → SQL Analysis → Power BI Dashboard → Business Insights**

The objective was to understand customer demographics, purchasing behaviour, product spending, and marketing campaign responses.

### Business Questions

- Who are the customers?
- What do they buy and how do they purchase?
- How do they respond to marketing campaigns?

---
## Business Problem

The dataset contains customer demographics, income, purchasing behaviour, product spending, website activity, and marketing campaign responses.

The objective was to clean and validate the data, analyse customer behaviour using SQL, build an interactive Power BI dashboard, and convert the findings into meaningful business insights.

## Dataset

The dataset contains **2,240 customer records and 29 columns**, covering:

- Customer demographics
- Income and household information
- Product spending
- Purchase behaviour
- Website activity
- Marketing campaign responses

## Key Business Insights
Customer Profile

Approximately 69% of customers are aged 50 or above, while Graduate customers form the largest education group with 1,330 customers.

Income & Spending

Average spending varies substantially across income groups:

Income Group	Average Spending
High	1,210.37
Medium	298.24
Low	97.51
Product Spending

Wines recorded the highest spending at 680,816, representing roughly half of spending across the six product categories.

Purchase Channels
Channel	Purchases
Store	12,970
Web	9,150
Catalog	5,963
Campaign Response

The overall campaign response rate was 14.91%, with 334 customers responding out of 2,240.

Campaign acceptance counts varied across the five campaigns. These are raw counts, so they should not be interpreted as direct campaign effectiveness rates.
